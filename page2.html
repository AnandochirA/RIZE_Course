More Places to Explore in Lynchburg

Lynchburg has many attractions for people interested in history, culture, nature, old school lifestyle, and family activities.

Historic Attractions

Old City Cemetery was established in 1806 and includes gardens, museums, and historic graves. Point of Honor is another historic location that offers visitors an opportunity to learn about Lynchburg's past.

Places to Explore

  1. Visit Thomas Jefferson's Poplar Forest.
  2. Explore the Old City Cemetery.
  3. Walk along the Blackwater Creek Trail.
  4. Visit the Lynchburg Museum.
  5. Explore Amazement Square.

Outdoor Attractions

Percival's Island Natural Area is another outdoor destination where visitors can enjoy a scenic trail experience near the James River. Lynchburg's riverfront and trail system provide opportunities for walking and biking.

Plan Your Visit

Lynchburg offers a mixture of historic sites, museums, outdoor recreation, and family-friendly attractions, making it an interesting place to explore.
